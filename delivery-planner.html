02 / Business scenarios

Where Databricks creates value

Databricks complements source applications by combining, governing and analysing their data. It does not replace the ERP transaction system.

SAP S/4HANAERP

Enterprise performance and process visibility

Combine finance, sales, procurement, inventory and manufacturing data with non-SAP sources for trusted cross-functional analysis.

  • Order-to-cash and margin analysis
  • Procure-to-pay and supplier performance
  • Inventory, demand and production insight
  • Finance close and reconciliation
Oracle Fusion / EBSERP

Finance and procurement analytics

Unify Oracle ERP data with budgets, operational systems and historical platforms without changing the system of record.

  • General ledger and management reporting
  • Spend and supplier analytics
  • Projects, assets and cost visibility
  • Reconciliation across Oracle environments
Dynamics 365 & other ERPERP

Cross-ERP reporting after growth or acquisition

Create shared definitions across Dynamics, NetSuite, Infor, legacy databases and spreadsheets while source systems remain operational.

  • Group-wide revenue and cost reporting
  • Common customer, supplier and product views
  • Migration profiling and validation
  • Historical reporting during ERP change
Supply chainPlanning

Demand, inventory and fulfilment decisions

Connect ERP transactions with warehouse, logistics, forecast and external signals to respond earlier.

  • Stock-out and excess inventory risk
  • On-time-in-full performance
  • Lead-time and supplier reliability
  • Forecast accuracy and scenario planning
CRM + commerceCustomer

Trusted customer and revenue intelligence

Join CRM, digital activity, orders, service and billing data to give commercial teams one governed view.

  • Customer 360 and segmentation
  • Churn and next-best-action models
  • Campaign and channel measurement
  • Sales pipeline and revenue forecasting
Manufacturing + IoTOperations

Quality, maintenance and production insight

Combine plant events and sensor data with ERP work orders, materials and quality records.

  • Predictive maintenance
  • Yield, scrap and downtime analysis
  • Batch and product traceability
  • Operational anomaly detection
Controls + riskGovernance

Continuous control and exception monitoring

Apply repeatable checks across high-volume transactions while keeping a traceable path to the source.

  • Duplicate payment and journal detection
  • Segregation-of-duties analytics
  • Master-data quality monitoring
  • Audit evidence and control dashboards
Documents + dataAI

Governed AI grounded in enterprise information

Prepare approved structured and unstructured data for assistants, forecasting and machine-learning workflows.

  • Document and policy search
  • Forecasting and optimisation
  • Case summarisation and routing
  • Model monitoring and governed access

Integration note: the correct extraction option depends on ERP version, licensing, approved interfaces, data volume, latency and the client's security architecture. Source access should be confirmed during discovery.
